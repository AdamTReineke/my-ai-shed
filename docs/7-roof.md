## Roof Sheathing — Zip System Envelope

Wall and roof sheathing form a continuous Zip System envelope:

- **Material**: 7/16" Zip System sheathing (wall + roof)
- **Wall panels**: 4×8 sheets cover 1.75" below subfloor + 87.25" wall frame + 7" truss end = 96" exactly
- **Roof panels**: 7/16" Zip on top of rafters, from eave (wall face) to ridge
- **Seams**: Zip System tape + roller at all panel joints
- **Nail schedule**: 6" o.c. edges, 12" o.c. field

### Standing Seam vs. Exposed Fastener
| | Standing Seam | Exposed Fastener |
|---|---|---|
| **Fasteners** | Hidden, never need re-driving | Exposed, rubber washers degrade over 20-25 yr |
| **Longevity** | 50+ years with no maintenance | May need re-fastening at 25 years |
| **Cost** | Higher | Lower |
| **DIY** | Harder (requires seaming tool or pro) | Easier |

For a 50-year building, standing seam is the better fit.

## Insulation (Hot Roof)

Insulation goes at the roof plane — no attic ventilation (unvented assembly).

### Cavity Insulation
- Polyiso or Rockwool batts friction-fit between truss top chords (3.5" cavity)
  - Rockwool ComfortBatt: R-15 in 3.5"
  - Polyiso: R-20+ in 3.5" (if cut from board stock)
- Hold in place with insulation support wire (zig-zag wire stapled between rafters)

### Optional Under-Rafter Boost
- Add 1–1.5" rigid foam board under rafters for ~R-20 to R-23 total
- Hold with cap screws into truss chords

### Interior Finish
- 1/2" CDX plywood over Rockwool (can be painted)
- No dedicated vapor barrier needed for zone 4C — latex paint is a Class III retarder and code-compliant
- For additional insurance on a hot roof: CertainTeed MemBrain smart vapor retarder (~$50-70) stapled under rafters before plywood

### Moisture Management Notes
With no ventilation above the insulation:
- Ensure thorough air sealing at the wall-to-roof connection
- Roof sheathing underlayment/barrier handles exterior moisture
- Mini-split or dehumidifier controls interior humidity
