# 11 — Trades & Mechanical

## HVAC

Mini-split sizing and placement are still open decisions.

- **Sizing:** 9,000 BTU (3/4 ton) is likely right for well-insulated 192 sf storage. 12,000 BTU if you want fast heat-up.
- **Indoor head placement:** Which wall?
- **Outdoor unit placement:** Which wall?
- **Dehumidifier:** In Redmond, a mini-split alone may not control humidity in shoulder seasons (50°F, 90% RH). A standalone dehumidifier with drain line through the floor is worth considering for protecting stored items.
