**Total wall thickness from interior stud face to siding face:** 5.5" (stud) + 7/16" + housewrap (negligible) + ¾" (furring) + 5/16" (HardiPlank) ≈ **7"**

This matters for door/window jamb extensions and trim depth.

## Layer Details

### 1. Interior Face
Bare studs. Optional future upgrade: ½" OSB or plywood for tool-hanging surface.

### 2. Insulation — Rockwool ComfortBatt R-23
- **Size:** 15.25" × 47" batts (fits 16" o.c. bays, friction-fit)
- **Thickness:** 5.5" (fills 2×6 cavity completely)
- **Fire rating:** Non-combustible, melts at 2150°F
- **Moisture:** Hydrophobic, does not absorb water, dries readily

### 3. Sheathing — 7/16" OSB
- **Panel size:** 4×8 sheets, installed vertically
- **Nailing:** 8d common (2.5"), 6" o.c. edges / 12" o.c. field, into studs
- **Provides:** Shear bracing (racking resistance) and nail base for siding

### 4. Weather-Resistive Barrier — Tyvek HomeWrap (or similar)
- **Install:** Horizontal courses, bottom-up, 6" overlap at horizontal seams
- **Tape:** Tyvek tape at all seams and penetrations
- **Sequence:** Applied over OSB before furring strips

### 5. Rainscreen — Furring Strips
- **Material:** 1×3 lumber (actual ¾" × 2½"), vertical over studs
- **Spacing:** 16" o.c. (aligned with studs for nailing)
- **Fastening:** #8 × 3" deck screws through housewrap/OSB into studs
- **Gap ventilation:**
  - **Bottom:** Cor-A-Vent SV-3 or similar bug screen strip, ¾" gap open for air intake
  - **Top:** Gap open behind fascia/soffit for exhaust (or perforated J-channel)
- **Purpose:** Drainage plane and drying gap behind siding — critical in PNW climate

### 6. Siding — HardiePlank Lap Siding
- **Plank size:** 8.25" wide × 12' long, 5/16" thick
- **Profile:** Cedarmill (textured) or smooth, 6.25" exposure (match house)
- **Nailing:** Blind-nailed 1" from top of plank
  - Nails must penetrate into studs (through ¾" furring + 7/16" OSB = 1.19" before stud)
  - Minimum 1¼" penetration into stud → minimum nail length: 1.19" + 0.31" (siding) + 1.25" = **2.75"** (use 3" stainless ring-shank)
- **Outside corners:** Aluminum outside siding corners (slip over plank ends at each corner, installed course-by-course)
  - 4 corners × 16 courses = **64 pieces** (sold individually, one per corner per course)
- **Starter strip:** ¼" × 1¼" HardiePlank starter strip at bottom, behind first course
- **Caulk:** Leave 1/8" gap at butt joints, caulk with paintable sealant
- **Paint:** Prime all cut edges; finish coat all faces (pre-primed planks)

## Flashing Details

### Bottom of Wall (Sill Flashing)
- Metal Z-flashing or peel-and-stick membrane at base of OSB, lapping over bottom plate
- Bug screen at bottom of rainscreen gap (Cor-A-Vent or equivalent)
- Maintain 1" minimum gap between siding and any horizontal surface

### Top of Wall
- Rainscreen gap vents at top behind fascia
- Housewrap laps under any roof underlayment or flashing

### Door Opening
- Flexible flashing tape (Vycor or similar) at R.O. jambs and head
- Head flashing: metal drip cap over door head casing, tucked under housewrap above
- Jamb/head trim must account for total buildup: ¾" furring + 5/16" siding = ~1-1/16" proud of housewrap plane
- Trim boards (1× PVC or HardieTrim) wrap jambs and head, siding butts to trim with 1/8" caulked gap

## Installation Sequence

1. **Insulate** stud bays (Rockwool friction-fit, no vapor barrier needed)
2. **Sheathe** with OSB — nail schedule: 6"/12"
3. **Wrap** with housewrap — bottom-up, tape all seams
4. **Flash** door opening — sill, jambs, head (in that order)
5. **Fur out** with 1×3 strips over studs, install bug screen at base
6. **Side** with HardiePlank — starter strip, then courses bottom-up
7. **Trim** door opening — PVC or HardieTrim, caulk siding-to-trim joints

## Plan: Individual Siding Pieces in CAD Model

### Goal
Replace the current monolithic siding slabs with individual HardiePlank pieces at real dimensions, with staggered butt joints, to produce a visual render and accurate cut list.

### Corner Geometry

Aluminum outside corner pieces wrap both wall surfaces at each corner. The siding planks on each wall stop at the corner trim — they don't extend past the building corner. The corner piece is ~1.5" wide on each face and covers the plank ends.

For modeling, we treat each wall's siding as running the full length of that wall's siding plane, with a small (1/8") gap inset from each corner for the aluminum corner piece overlap. The E/W siding wraps over the N/S wall ends (E/W cladding is the "outside" layer at corners).

**Siding plane lengths** (face-to-face at corners):
- **N/S walls**: `shed_length` = 192" (siding sits on the N/S furring, which is flush with the framing ends)
- **E/W walls**: `shed_width + 2 × (osb + furring)` = 144 + 2 × (7/16 + 3/4) = 144 + 2.375 = **146.375"**
  (The siding wraps from south furring face to north furring face, covering the N/S wall buildup)

With aluminum corner inset (~1/8" gap each end):
- **N/S plank length**: 192" − 2 × 1/8" = **191.75"** → needs 2 pieces per course (144" max plank)
- **E/W plank length**: 146.375" − 2 × 1/8" = **146.125"** → needs 2 pieces per course (144" max plank)

### Butt Joint Rules
1. **Plank stock**: 12' (144") HardiePlank
2. **Butt joints**: 1/8" gap, caulked. Must land on a furring strip (stud location) for nailing support
3. **Stagger**: Adjacent courses must offset butt joints by at least 24" (2 stud bays)
4. **Door opening**: Planks are cut to the door trim — no butt joints within the R.O.

### Stagger Strategy

**N/S walls (191.75"):** Each course needs two pieces. Alternate between two patterns:
- **Pattern A** (odd courses): Full 144" plank from left + cut piece (~47.75") on right
- **Pattern B** (even courses): Shifted so the butt joint moves ~48" (3 stud bays). E.g., ~96" from left + ~95.75" from right

The butt joint must land on a furring strip. Candidate stud positions on N/S walls:
`14.5, 30.5, 46.5, 62.5, 78.5, 94.5, 110.5, 126.5, 142.5, 158.5, 174.5`

Good joint positions (near midpoints, on furring):
- Pattern A joint at **142.5"** (full plank: 142.375", remainder: 49.25")
- Pattern B joint at **94.5"** (piece 1: 94.375", piece 2: 97.25")

This gives a 48" stagger between adjacent courses — well over the 24" minimum.

**E/W walls (146.125"):** Each course needs one full plank + a short piece. Stud positions on E/W walls (in cladding local coords, shifted by `stud_depth` to account for wrapping):
`5.5, 21.5, 37.5, 53.5, 69.5, 85.5, 101.5, 117.5, 133.5`

Good joint positions:
- Pattern A joint at **133.5"** (full plank: 133.375", remainder: 12.625")
- Pattern B joint at **85.5"** (piece 1: 85.375", piece 2: 60.625")

**North wall door region:** Courses 1–12 (below door top) are split by the door R.O. The pieces on each side of the door are independent — each side's planks run from the corner to the door trim, or from the door trim to the other corner. No butt joints needed on the short side (door-to-east-corner = 13.5"). The long side (west-corner-to-door = 145.375") needs a butt joint, staggered like the south wall.

### Implementation Plan

#### Step 1: Node.js Cut List Tool (`tools/siding-cuts.js`)

Write a tool that computes every plank piece for all four walls:

**Inputs** (hardcoded from dimensions):
- Wall lengths, door position, stud/furring positions
- Plank stock length (144"), exposure (6.25"), corner gap (1/8")
- Stagger patterns per wall

**Output** (stdout):
- Per-wall, per-course list of pieces: `{wall, course, piece, left_x, right_x, length, stock_usage}`
- Summary: total planks needed, offcut inventory, cut list grouped by unique lengths
- JSON mode (`--json`) for consumption by OpenSCAD

The tool should also output an OpenSCAD-consumable data file (`siding_cuts.json` or inline `.scad` data) with arrays of `[wall_id, course, x_start, x_end]` tuples.

#### Step 2: Update `wall_cladding.scad`

Replace `siding_courses()` module with `siding_planks()` that reads the computed cut positions:

- Each plank is an individual cuboid at its exact position and length
- Butt joint gaps (1/8") are visible between adjacent pieces
- Door-region planks stop at the door trim line
- Corner gaps are visible at wall ends

Since OpenSCAD can't read JSON at render time, the tool should output a `.scad` file (e.g., `siding_data.scad`) containing the plank arrays as OpenSCAD variables:

```
// Generated by tools/siding-cuts.js — do not edit
// south_siding[course] = [[x_start, x_end], ...]
south_siding = [
    [[0.125, 142.375], [142.625, 191.625]],  // course 0 (pattern A)
    [[0.125, 94.375], [94.625, 191.625]],     // course 1 (pattern B)
    ...
];
```

#### Step 3: Verify and Iterate

- Render in OpenSCAD, visually inspect stagger pattern and corner alignment
- Confirm total plank count matches material estimate
- Print cut list for shop use
