# 10 — Stairs & Landing

## Entry Landing

The door is on the north wall at the east end and opens inward. Since it opens inward, the landing doesn't need to be as deep as for an outswing door, but **36" deep minimum** is still needed for comfortable entry.

The site slopes ~28" east to west, so the east end (door side) is closest to grade. The steps and landing may only need 1-2 risers depending on final pier/floor elevation — confirm after floor framing is complete.
