# 8 — Fastener Schedule

All fasteners in contact with PT lumber or exterior exposure must be **stainless steel** or **hot-dip galvanized (HDG)**. ACQ-treated wood corrodes plain steel and electroplated (zinc-plated) fasteners. Stainless is preferred for longevity (50-year design life); HDG is acceptable for interior/protected connections.

**Nail gun note:** Most connections below call for hand-nailing or a framing nailer. For 16d HDG nails, a full-head framing nailer (e.g., Bostitch F21PL) is ideal. Ring-shank nails for sheathing can use a coil nailer. Verify your nailer accepts HDG/SS nail strips before buying.

## 3. Beam Lamination — 2 beams × 16'

3-ply built-up beam: 2×10 + 3×10 + 2×10 (5.5" × 9.25"), per [2-beam.md](2-beam.md).

| Fastener | Pattern | Per beam | Total (×2) | Notes |
|----------|---------|----------|------------|-------|
| GRK RSS 5/16" × 3-1/8" structural screw | Both faces, 12" o.c. staggered | ~30 | ~60 | Drives from each face through outer ply into center ply |

**Pattern:** Stagger screws on each face so they alternate at 6" effective spacing along the beam. Drive from both sides — each screw goes through one 2× outer ply (1.5") into the 3× center ply (2.5"), for 4" total. The 3-1/8" screw provides full engagement.

**Alternative (budget):** 10d HDG nails (3" × 0.148") from both faces at 12" o.c. staggered, clinched where they penetrate through. Screws are preferred — they provide better clamping force and are easier to install accurately.

## Notes

- **PT compatibility:** All fasteners touching pressure-treated wood must be SS or HDG. ACQ treatment corrodes plain steel and electro-galvanized (EG) coatings. This applies to: pier hardware, beam screws, joist ties, rim joist nails, bottom plate nails, subfloor nails, and blocking nails.
- **Interior framing exception:** Wall studs, top plates, headers, and trusses are standard (non-PT) KD lumber protected from weather. Standard bright nails are acceptable here.
- **Simpson fastener rules:** Always use the fastener type and size specified by Simpson for their connectors. Never substitute screws for nails in hurricane ties or joist hangers unless using Simpson SD connector screws.
- **Nail lengths:** 16d common = 3.5", 10d common = 3", 8d common = 2.5". "Common" has a thicker shank than "box" or "sinker" — use common for structural connections.
